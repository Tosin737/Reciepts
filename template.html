Cheating arena

Fifty honest sources answer true or false on 300 claims. An attacker adds Sybil sources that all give the wrong answer on 30 targeted claims. Each scoring method turns the sources into weights, and a weighted vote decides each claim. The chart tracks how often the vote is right on the targeted claims as Sybils are added.

    Agent ledger

    A buyer agent records what it saw and did. The seller agent co-signs the agreement, and a witness signs checkpoints. Pick an attack, then choose how many protections the verifier has. Everything below uses real ECDSA signatures and SHA-256 in your browser.

    Verifier checks

    An insider holds the log owner's signing key, so re-signing a rewritten chain is possible for them. An outsider has no key.

    Evidence meter

    Paste any text, or pick a specimen. The meter splits it into sentences and sorts each one by how far it can be tied to checkable evidence. It reads the wording only. It does not fetch or verify anything.

    How the sorting works
    • Evidence attached means the sentence carries a pointer a machine can follow: a link, a document number, a citation, or a named source with "according to".
    • Checkable, no pointer means the sentence has figures or a named person or body, so evidence could be looked up, but nothing in the text says where.
    • Too vague to bind means a general claim with no figures or names.
    • Opinion or forecast means a value judgement, a hedge, or a prediction, which cannot be checked against a record today.
    • Procedural lines such as "meeting adjourned" are left out of the percentages.

    Method

    The arena world

    Each honest source has a hidden accuracy between 0.55 and 0.92 and answers about half of the claims. Honest sources cite other sources, preferring accurate ones. Sybils answer every targeted claim wrongly and answer other claims with the accuracy and copying behaviour of the chosen attack. Sybil sources cite each other, and honest sources link to them at a rate set by the attack. Targeted claims are 10 percent of all claims.

    Scoring methods

    • No tool. Every source gets the same weight.
    • Citation rank. PageRank over the citation graph.
    • Agreement with majority. Weight rises with how often a source matches the plain majority.
    • Truth discovery. Weights and verdicts are updated together for 10 rounds, so sources that agree with the current verdict gain weight.
    • Random audit. Ground truth is checked on a random sample of claims, and each source is scored by the lower bound of its audited accuracy.
    • Targeted audit with independence. Half the audit budget is random. The other half goes to the claims where the vote is most split. Sources that agree on at least 90 percent of 30 or more shared claims are grouped, and each group shares one voice.

    Attacks

      Metrics and assumptions

      • The headline metric is accuracy of the weighted vote on the 30 targeted claims, averaged over 30 seeds. A tie counts as half right.
      • Audits assume ground truth can be obtained for the sampled claims. That is the expensive step in practice, and its cost is the budget slider.
      • Sybils always answer targeted claims while honest sources answer half of all claims. Reducing that gap would make every method look stronger.
      • Results depend on this world. The 90 percent grouping threshold and the 0.85 to 0.95 camouflage accuracies were set before the runs and not tuned afterwards.

      Ledger design

      Each record holds a sequence number, the previous record's hash, a kind, a body, and an optional counterparty signature. The record hash covers all of that, and the agent signs the hash. The counterparty signs the record content, which includes the previous hash, so a co-signed agreement also pins everything before it. A witness signs the pair (position, head hash) and refuses a second head for a position it has already signed.

      Reproducing

      The simulation, ledger and meter are plain scripts with seeded randomness. The build runs them in Node and writes the numbers shown in Findings. This page also runs the same code live, so the Arena and Ledger tabs can recompute results and compare them with the build.

      Next experiment: real agents

      Give language-model agents 300 claims with source answers, once with raw votes and once with each score attached. Add Sybils as in the arena and compare accuracy on the targeted claims. If an agent trusts a gameable score, the gap between the two arms will show it.
